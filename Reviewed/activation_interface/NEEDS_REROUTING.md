# activation_interface — NEEDS RE-ROUTING (2026-10-12)

## Why
The routed boards (`Review/activation_interface`, 2026-09-02, and `WIP/Francesco/cell_floor_board`,
2026-09-03) predate the change that brings out every fin's full bipolar coil pair. The final
aft_end_board (`Review/aft_end_board`) below and fin_ble_board (`Reviewed/fin_ble_board2`) above both
carry the 12 extra nets — FIN1_AOUT2, FIN1_BOUT1, FIN1_BOUT2, FIN2_AOUT2, FIN2_BOUT1, FIN2_BOUT2, FIN3_AOUT2, FIN3_BOUT1, FIN3_BOUT2, FIN4_AOUT2, FIN4_BOUT1, FIN4_BOUT2 — and they have to pass straight up through activation_interface
and cell_floor_board. The old boards carry none of them, and a check showed it is NOT fixable by
re-assigning land nets: on activation the 12 nets have no bottom→top copper path at all (29
pass-through paths exist, 40 nets must pass), and with straight-through spacers the forced land
nets would land on the ground plane, the 3V3 supply and the ACTIVATE_HV copper. Both boards need
re-routing against the land fields below.

## This copy
Generated by `generate_stackup.py` (dist/maximalist, 2026-10-12): component placement locked as on
the routed board, LGA land fields matching the final neighbours —
- aft_end side: pinned from `Review/aft_end_board` (`application/src/smash/data/aft_end_board_ee_lands.json`)
- fin_ble side: through the battery column, matching `Reviewed/fin_ble_board2`
The generated spacers (`dist/maximalist/spacer_aft_end_board_activation_interface`,
`spacer_activation_interface_cell_floor_board`, `spacer_battery_1..6`) mate these fields and
supersede the reviewed spacers in `Review/spacer_*` (those match the old boards only).
Net names follow the current netlist (FIRE_* → ACTIVATE_*, ACTIVATE_ARM → ACTIVATION_UNLOCK).

State of the copy: Francesco's original copper (791 tracks, 88 vias, 4 plane zones: FLEX_GND
In1/In12, 3V3 In2, COMP_5V In11) is still imported verbatim under its OLD nets, so it no longer
matches the re-assigned lands. KiCad DRC (errors): 94 shorts, 63 unconnected, 199 hole-clearance,
117 solder-mask-bridge, 16 clearance. Keep the component placement; re-route the land fan-out and
the pass-through nets (including 12 new bottom→top paths for the fin pairs). Note the plane zones:
several re-assigned lands sit inside the FLEX_GND / 3V3 / COMP_5V plane areas. The gerbers in this
folder are generated from this state and are NOT fab data.
