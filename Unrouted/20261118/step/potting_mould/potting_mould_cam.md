# Potting mould / clamp fixture (generated)

The LDS sleeve IS the potting container (it stays on as the outer skin); this fixture clamps its two half-shells closed during vacuum potting and closes the ends.

- Clamp: two half-tubes (split on x = 0, across the sleeve seams), OD Ø50, bore = sleeve OD + 0.05 mm. Material: PTFE (prototype) or hard-anodised Al with a PTFE-type coating (production) — no release agent (it would spoil LDS / potting adhesion).
- Bottom cap (aft): fill port Ø3.0 under the 0° feed groove.
- Top cap (nose): vent port Ø3.0 over the 180° vent groove.
- Open: radar 77 GHz air-gap dam on the nose cap side; sleeve-end seals (silicone gaskets); clamp bolts.
