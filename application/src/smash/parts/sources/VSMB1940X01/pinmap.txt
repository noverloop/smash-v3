K=1, A=2

Pad 1 = cathode (footprint left, silk tick), pad 2 = anode — same K/A
convention as the LTST-C190 LEDs and the VBPW34FAS. Unlike the
photodiode's DS, DS 81933 labels the leads explicitly in the package
top view (anode left / cathode right in the drawing's orientation) and
the blister-tape drawing marks which terminal faces the sprocket row —
transfer that orientation onto the assembly drawing.
