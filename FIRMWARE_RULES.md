# Firmware rules

Hardware constraints that only firmware can honour. Append-only: add new rules at the
end with the next number; when a rule stops applying, mark it **RETIRED** with the
reason instead of deleting it. Each rule names the part/net, what firmware must do,
why, and where it comes from.

| # | Area | Rule | Why | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | PMIC (power_board) | Provision the STPMIC25 NVM once, on the bench: BUCK7, LDO2, LDO4 to rank 0 (unused rails); BUCK4 pull-down = fast. Lock the NVM afterwards. | Factory NVM turns BUCK7/LDO2/LDO4 on with no load; COMP_3V3 must discharge with VDDA18AON on power-down. | DS14278 Table 1, AN6198 |
| 2 | Power-off (MP25 + WBA) | Every power-off goes MP25 → PMIC SWOFF over I²C. PWR_HOLD / QHOLD2 are released only after the PMIC is OFF. The WBA pack-undervoltage cutoff asks the MP25 to shut down; it never drops QMAIN while the PMIC is on. | A COMP_5V collapse drops VDD while VDDA18AON / VDDCORE are up: STM32MP25 DS §6.3.2 "damage could occur". | STM32MP25 DS §6.3.2 |
| 3 | DDR (TF-A) | Before DDR init enable BUCK6 = 1.35 V (rank 0 by default). Leave the PMIC's VREFDDR off: the DDR3L VREF is a local 1k/1k divider from COMP_1V35. | DDR3L VREF follows VDDQ. | DS14278 Table 1 |
| 4 | MP25 USB | Keep USB3DR and USBH disabled in the device tree (or measure LDO1 first). | VDDA18USB is on PMIC LDO1 (20 mA) with VDDA18AON; the HS PHY draws up to 18.5 mA. | DS14278 Table 9, MP25 DS |
| 5 | MP25 EXTI | EXTI lines are shared per pin number (max 2 sources per line on the MP25); new interrupt pins must keep `stm32_functions.INTERRUPTS` within capacity. | Two EXTI controllers. | DS14285 §3.21 |
| 6 | MP25 boot (probe) | Serial boot of a blank NAND: dev probe drives PB13 **low** (or S_DEV_MP25_BOOT) — BOOT0/BOOT2 high = serial-NAND boot. | BOOT[3:0]=0101 serial NAND, 0000 UART/USB. | DS14285 Table 5 |
| 7 | Dev probe (U535) | Keep the probe's SWD/UART/NRST/BOOT pins Hi-Z until the target's NRST reads high; only then drive them. | The bus switches enable on 3V3_AON, not on the MP25/fin rails — driving an unpowered target back-powers it. | audit round 2 |
| 8 | Dev probe CAN | Trim HSI16 against the CRS-locked HSI48, or keep the CAN-FD data phase low. | No crystal on the probe: HSI16 ±1 %. | audit round 2 |
| 9 | Dev bench supply | Read the PAC1941 VBUS before enabling a power path (refuse FLAT > 4.3 V, TWR > 6 V) and keep reading it while a path is on: switch the path off on any excursion. The hardware cut-off (U_DEV_OVP, 5.9 V) is the backstop. | A mis-set bench supply overstresses the BAT_PROT converters above ≈ 6.7 V and, above ≈ 16 V, breaks Q_ISO down into the primary cells. | audit round 2; electrical audit |
| 10 | WBA ↔ MP25 | While the MP25 is off (COMP_3V3 down), the WBA puts its UART TX, HCI MISO/IRQ pins in analog or low. | They back-drive the unpowered MP25 and break the Standby budget. | audit round 1 F5 |
| 11 | I²C2 multi-master | The WBA never addresses the PMIC (0x33); TF-A does bus recovery before talking to the PMIC. Decide which core owns I²C2. | PMIC shares I²C2 with sensors and two masters. | DS14278 §5.5.1 |
| 12 | AWR1843 reset | Drive AWR_NRESET (PI6) low before RADAR_EN; release ≥ 5 ms after the radar rails are good. | NRESET's RC releases before the rails ramp. | SWRS236C §6.10.3 |
| 13 | AWR1843 TX | Use at most two transmitters at once in 1.3 V RF-supply mode. | Three TX need the 1.0 V LDO-bypass mode. | SWRS236C Table 6-1 |
| 14 | Camera (VB1943) | Keep CAM_NRST (PA9) open-drain and low until LDO5 (VANA) is up ≥ 100 µs; never drive it push-pull high. | RESETN is a 1.8 V input; VANA is rank 0. | DS14880 §4.2.1 |
| 15 | LR2021 TCXO | Set the TCXO start-up delay ≥ 3.5 ms. If the TG2016 (quartz, DNP) is fitted instead of the SiT5356, set VTCXO = 3.0 V. | SiT5356 start-up 3.5 ms max; TG2016 is a 2.8–3.3 V part. | SiT5356 DS Table 2; TG2016SMN code "M" |
| 16 | ADF4351 | Power down the auxiliary output stage (RFOUTB±, left open on the board). | DS: the aux output can be powered down when unused. | ADF4351 DS p13 |
| 17 | Piezo launch detect | COMP_OUT (MAX9064, push-pull) goes **high** when the piezo exceeds 0.2 V: use a rising-edge interrupt. | MAX9064 non-inverting, push-pull. | MAX9060–64 DS Rev 11 |
| 18 | Parachute cutter | Burn pulses ≤ 10 ms with a retry gap. | BAS40-02L steering diodes: 120 mA DC, 200 mA for ≤ 10 ms. | Infineon BAS40 DS |
| 19 | Probe console | The probe's USART1 listens on PA10 (MP25 console TX) and sends on PA9. | Pin assignment after the TX/RX fix. | generate_devboard.py |
| 20 | BMM350 axes | The land is the datasheet top view rotated 90°: map the sensing axes accordingly. | Footprint orientation. | library check |
| 21 | WBA standby (3V3 off) | While 3V3 / COMP_3V3 are off, the WBA holds every line into a switched-rail part low or Hi-Z: XV_LO_LE/CE, LR_DTC_SCL/SDA (ADF4351), XV_MIX_EN (LTC5552), I2C2 SCL/SDA (IMU1 on 3V3 — no I²C2 traffic in Standby), and the MP25 host link (UART, HCI SPI, WBA_BOOT0). | Each pin drives a part whose own supply is off (back-power through its clamps). | audits D2 (`validators/audits.py`) |
| 22 | **RETIRED** (superseded by #24: the shock wake shares MAG_WAKE_N, PB11 is MUXOUT again) — WBA wake on shock | COMP_OUT (MAX9064, push-pull, on 3V3_AON) is on WBA PB11: in Standby the WBA sleeps in a Stop mode (PB11 and PC14 are EXTI, not PWR_WKUP pins) with a rising-edge EXTI11 on PB11; on that edge it closes QMAIN / holds power and boots the system to **Ready**. Firmware tells it from the magnetic wake (MAG_WAKE_N, level held while the magnet is present) by pulse length. | Piezo launch shock must bring the round to Ready. | user decision 2026-11-24 |
| 23 | **RETIRED** (PB11 reads MUXOUT again, see #24) — ADF4351 lock detect | MUXOUT is no longer read by the WBA (PB11 is the shock wake): set MUXOUT to tri-state (or digital lock detect for TP_XV_LO_MUX at bring-up) and rely on the programmed settle time. | Pin reassignment. | user decision 2026-11-24 |
| 24 | WBA wake on shock | A launch shock pulls MAG_WAKE_N (WBA PC14) low through Q_SHOCK_WAKE (COMP_OUT drives its gate). In Standby the WBA sleeps in a Stop mode with a falling-edge EXTI14 on PC14; on wake it tells the source apart — magnet = level held low while the magnet is present, piezo = a short pulse (the MP25, once up, also sees COMP_OUT on PE11) — and on a shock closes QMAIN / holds power and boots to **Ready**. | Piezo launch shock must bring the round to Ready; no WBA pin spare. | user decision 2026-11-24 |
| 25 | Dev probe rail monitor | U_DEV_MON1 (0x11), MON2 (0x12), MON3 (0x13) on I²C1 with the PAC1941 (0x10). Reference = AVDD = DEV_3V3: read the PAC1941 / a known rail to calibrate. Scale: series-only inputs ×1; COMP_5V, COMP_5V_RAW, REG_IN_MAIN, BAT_PROT, XV_5V ×2; ACTIVATE_HV ×13.20 (1 M / 82 k). MON1: COMP_3V3, COMP_1V8_AON, COMP_0V82, COMP_0V9, COMP_1V8, COMP_1V35, COMP_2V8, COMP_5V. MON2: COMP_5V_RAW, REG_IN_MAIN, 3V3, 3V3_AON, TMR_VDD, MAG_1V8, ACTIVATE_HV, AWR_1V2. MON3: AWR_1V3, AWR_1V8, PMIC_PWR_OK, PWR_HOLD, RADAR_EN, BAT_PROT (×2), NRST, XV_5V (×2) (AIN0–7). Sample ≥ 1 kSPS per channel for the power-up order; each input has a ≈ 1 ms RC. | Bring-up logging and pass/fail against BRING_UP.md. | generate_devboard.py; ADS7138 DS Table 2 |
| 26 | Dev probe power paths | Never enable a tower power path while DEV_3V3 is off, and keep the probe powered while the tower is (USB attached). | The rail monitor inputs are clamped to DEV_3V3: a live tower rail back-feeds the strip through the 100 k series resistors and corrupts the standby current reading. | generate_devboard.py |
| 27 | WBA clocks / radios | BLE and LoRa run only in Ready (3V3 on). The WBA uses HSE32 (Y_WBA_HSE) only while 3V3 is up: switch SYSCLK/radio clock to HSI before 3V3 goes down, and never select HSE in Standby. The S1 antenna switch is unpowered with 3V3 off. | Y_WBA_HSE (6-7 mA) and U_ANTSW_S1 (65-110 µA) moved off 3V3_AON to keep Standby within budget. | user decision 2026-11-25 |
| 28 | WBA external-power detect | External power = BAT_SENSE (WBA PA3, REG_IN_MAIN / 2) above ≈ 2.2 V (BAT_PROT > ≈ 4.4 V; the cells reach 4.07 V at most, the fixture / USB put BAT_PROT at ≈ 4.7-4.8 V). Valid only with QMAIN closed (Ready). Use it where a feature is restricted to external power (e.g. BLE). | No dedicated detect line reaches an MCU (MP25 PC10 is strapped high; no spare WBA pin). | user decision 2026-11-25 |
| 29 | BLE update on battery, back to Shelf | Wake (magnet → Standby → Ready, or the TMR coded shelf wake) → Ready (3V3 up: BLE + S1 switch powered) → update (WBA: FUOTA on its own flash; MP25: changed partitions only, relayed over the HCI SPI link into NAND; images signed, checked by secure boot, authenticated BLE session only) → shut down: MP25 SWOFF to the PMIC first (#2), then the WBA opens QMAIN and releases WBA_AON_HOLD. Before releasing, wait until MAG_WAKE_N reads high (magnet gone): the TMR shelf sensor (0.7 mT) pulls the power gates directly and would wake the round again. Keep the session inside the Ready budget (≈ 1 h per wake, battery sim). | Field update without external power, ending in Shelf (≈ 1 µA). | user decision 2026-11-25 |
| 30 | Cutter boost enable | ACTIVATE_BOOST_EN (MP25 PG10, 100 k pull-down) is low except around a cut: drive it high ≥ 5 ms before PARACHUTE_CUTTER_SET (47 nF soft-start ≈ 4 ms; check ACTIVATE_CONFIRM ≈ 3.27 V), low again when the cut sequence ends. | No 33 V on the cutter during handling; the TPS61175 + bleed + monitor cost ≈ 14 mA at the pack while enabled. | electrical audit (peak/brown-out) |
| 31 | Fin / cutter / chirp interlock | Never step a fin while a cutter burn is on. Below a 3.0 V pack (BAT_SENSE < 1.5 V), do not step a fin inside a radar active frame either. | Fins + chirp + cutter at a 2.6 V pack need 2.66 A per LTC3124 phase (2.5 A min limit). | LTC3124 DS (3124f); battery sim |
| 32 | PMIC brown-out | Set VIN_FLT_CNT_MAX deliberately at provisioning. At boot the MP25 reads the PMIC RESTART_SR: after a VINOK_Fall restart it does not re-enter descent tasks (log, report, go to the safe state). On shutdown the WBA opens QMAIN anyway if the MP25 does not acknowledge within 1 s. | A sagging pack can trip VINOK_Fall (3.5 V on COMP_5V), restart, trip again and latch FAIL_SAFE_LOCK; the WBA has no PMIC status line. | DS14278 Table 1, §5.4.5 |
| 33 | Radar rail dips | After any AWR rail fault or error / ready loss, re-run the rule-12 reset sequence (AWR_NRESET low, rails, ≥ 5 ms, release). | The radar bucks' UVLO (2.1-2.3 V falling) can drop the AWR rails during a cutter + chirp dip while AWR_NRESET stays high. | TPS62822 / TPS62088 DS |
| 34 | Health coil | Coil pulses ≤ 1 ms, duty ≤ 3 %. | 4 × 47 Ω 0603 (0.1 W each): ≈ 0.17-0.19 W per resistor during a pulse. | electrical audit |
| 35 | Production debug lock | Before a unit leaves production: MP25 closed (BSEC / OTP debug lock), WBA55 and U545 at RDP level 2 or their product-state lock. | The SWD lines sit on the public aft crown. | electrical audit (external interfaces) |
| 36 | PMIC buck current limits | Program BUCK2's IOUT (NVM) to the lowest setting that covers the measured VDDCORE peak, so its IBCKLIM (DS14278 Table 15) stays below L_PMIC_BUCK2's 2.7 A saturation current. Same for BUCK1 (COMP_0V9) and BUCK6 (COMP_1V35) against 2.7 A (IOUT ≤ 1.0 A → IBCKLIM 2.1 A typ; BUCK6's default depends on the part number) and BUCK4 / BUCK5 against 1.2 A. | At the factory 2.0 A setting the current limit (3.3 A typ) is above the inductor's Isat; a fault would saturate it before the limit acts. | DS14278 Tables 15-16; TDK TMS201210ALM |
| 37 | WBA battery sense + AON hold | (a) Every BAT_SENSE conversion computes VDDA from VREFINT / VREFINT_CAL (DS14127 §3.20.3) — never a fixed 3.3 V: below a ≈ 3.35 V pack the AON LDO is in dropout and VDDA (the ADC reference) follows the pack, so a fixed-reference reading stays at ≈ 3.37 V and rules #28 / #31 and the 2.5 V pack cutoff would never trip. (b) Drive PB15 (WBA_AON_HOLD) high first thing in startup, before RAM init. | The AON LDO runs from the pack; AON_EN is held by PB15 (or PWR_HOLD) through OR diodes. | schematic audit R3 |
| 38 | WBA ↔ LR2021 in pack dips | Do not start LR2021 SPI transactions during a cutter burn, a fin step or a radar active frame; check BUSY / status after each command and retry on error. | The WBA runs from 3V3_AON, which follows the pack below ≈ 3.35 V; in ms-class dips its VOH can fall under the LR2021 VIH (0.7 × 3.3 V = 2.31 V). | schematic audit R3 |
| 39 | Radar boot policy (configurable) | A provisioning / mission parameter selects when RADAR_EN goes high: **at wake** (radar ready instantly; ≈ 0.18 A idle on the 1.2 / 1.8 V rails for the whole Ready hold) or **at launch / drop** (first shock-wake or descent trigger; ≈ 0.2-0.5 s to the first frame, estimate). With boot-at-launch, store the boot-time RF calibrations in the radar flash once (mmWave SDK calibration save / restore) and restore them at each boot to shorten it. Follow rules #12 / #33 for the reset sequence either way. | Battery sim: boot-at-launch raises the descent budget from 410 to 593 s (fresh pack, +22 °C), from 92 to 258 s (fresh, −20 °C) and from 83 to 263 s (10-year pack, +22 °C). | user decision 2026-11-25; `python -m smash.sim.battery --radar-off-in-ready` |
| 40 | Dev probe power status | Read DEV_INPROT_FAULT (PA15, low = LTC4365 input fault: reversed / outside ≈ 2.5-6.5 V), DEV_OVP (PB10, high = feed > 5.9 V) and DEV_OVP_FLAT (PC13, high = feed > 4.37 V) and report them with every power-path change; the tower-presence input DEV_TWR_VDD_SENSE now reads ≈ 2.13 V for a live 3V3_AON (1 k series on the tower side) — threshold ≤ 2.0 V. | The hardware cuts power on its own; firmware must say why. | bring-up review 2026-11-25 |
